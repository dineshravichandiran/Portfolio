01 — What I Do
Daily operations.
Pune, India

Three years keeping enterprise SaaS platforms online for 50+ Fortune 500 customers — the person who gets paged at 2 a.m. and has it resolved before the business day starts. Here's what that actually looks like, day to day.

01

Incident Response

Handle production incidents across AWS and Azure for 50+ Fortune 500 customers via on-call rotation. Triage, resolve directly where possible, or escalate to NOC Central and Incident Management teams. Lead outage coordination across App, DB, and Network teams during high-severity events.

02

Root Cause Analysis

Investigate failures by analyzing application logs in Sumo Logic, AWS CloudWatch, and Windchill logs to identify root cause. Document findings, then resolve directly or escalate to the right team. Produce trend analysis on recurring issues to drive permanent fixes.

03

Linux, AKS & Containers

Troubleshoot production Linux servers (200+) — CPU, memory, disk, networking, Apache/Tomcat. Pod- and node-level troubleshooting on Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS) using kubectl and k9s — node health, namespaces, container status, and log analysis. Act on alerts like CrashLoopBackOff, OOMKilled, pod restarts, node scale-downs, and unhealthy containers.

04

Windchill PLM Operations

Administer PTC Windchill PDMLink at the operational level — verify service and daemon configuration alignment, set up and start Windchill services, and bring up dependent CAD and third-party applications customers rely on. Manage Apache DS, Red Hat DS, and Tomcat. Start, stop, and restart services as needed to keep customer environments stable.

05

Monitoring & Alert Lifecycle

Own the alert lifecycle in Zabbix — monitor auto-resolved alerts, manually resolve those that don't clear, and verify and close alerts once underlying issues (including customer-side) are resolved. Set up and validate monitoring for customer go-lives, ensuring all monitoring services are active, enabled, and fixed where needed.

06

Ownership & Knowledge

Raise and manage change requests following ITIL change management, and perform scheduled maintenance applying remediation recommendations. Authored 10+ published runbooks that the team now relies on. Collaborate with Technical Architects on infrastructure recovery and major incident response, including during large-scale industry-wide outage events.

Zabbix Monitoring Lab — Platform Deep-Dive

Self-built labs · Platform-level Zabbix skills beyond day-to-day alert tuning

Production work covers alert tuning and triage — this self-hosted Zabbix lab (Docker) was built to go deeper into the platform-engineering side I don't touch daily: discovery rules, preprocessing, escalation logic, RBAC, and the API. Terraform-based provisioning is the next iteration I'm building toward, extending the same repeatable, version-controlled approach to monitoring-as-code.

Problem
Needed real reps on LLD tuning, dependent items, proxy architecture, and least-privilege RBAC before being asked to design a Zabbix estate from scratch.
Action

Result
Cut discovered filesystem items by more than half via LLD filters, eliminated alert cascades with trigger dependencies, and can now speak concretely to nodata() triggers, proxy buffer sizing, and RBAC design.
